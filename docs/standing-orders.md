# Standing Orders

Standing orders use D6 option A: a normal Dossier with canonical slug `standing-orders`. This repository intentionally does not invent or check in the principal's personal principles. Create/populate that Dossier in the principal's Dossier store, record each adopted order under `## Decisions` with date, attribution, rationale, and evidence, and let Team Sync carry it to the VM.

The principal owns the content. Agent edits to `## Decisions` are captured as `agent_proposal` conflicts and require human resolution (B22); agents must not treat draft proposals as adopted orders. Until populated, no standing orders have been supplied. The absence must remain visible rather than being interpreted as an empty grant of authority.

D6 option B (a separate `orders.md` document and `dossier_orders` tool) is not adopted: the normal Dossier already provides revisions, audit, conflict handling, and sync.
